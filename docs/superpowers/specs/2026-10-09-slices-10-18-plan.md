  delete), `/admin/teams` (+ details, insert).
- **Exists:** schema, `adminGate`, roles; no pages; no `isSuperadmin`, no emailed-code
  confirmation (R-26).
- **Rulings:** R-89, R-90, R-91, R-92; R-26 amended (results manager enters standings
  facts).
- **Lead decisions:**
  - a round with games cannot be deleted;
  - the tournament `status` field is dropped (R-21);
  - a rate is any whole number from 1;

  - every write recalculates (R-16, R-37);
  - inserting a game seeds every member's rows and a blank odds row.
